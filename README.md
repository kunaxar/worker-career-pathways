# Career pathways (prototype)

A Flutter web prototype: pick your trade, say where you are now, and see the real next step with the credentials and hours it takes.

Flow: choose a trade > mark where you are on the ladder > "Your next step" card shows what you have and what is missing > "Plan this step" lists each requirement with its source and where to start > Pay tab shows the trade's national pay range.

## Research basis
- Steps, training length, licensing and advancement: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook pages for Electricians, Carpenters, Plumbers/Pipefitters/Steamfitters, Welders. Pay is May 2025 (10th percentile, median, 90th percentile).
- Apprenticeship hours: BLS Career Outlook (about 2,000 on-the-job hours and at least 144 hours of instruction per year).
- NCCER Electrical levels 1-4 (672.5 hours including Core), OSHA Outreach 10/30-hour cards, AWS Certified Welder and Certified Welding Inspector.
- Not validated with workers. Hypotheses are listed in the Research tab.

## What is simulated
- The worker (Marcus, an electrician apprentice) is fictional. Ticks are kept in the browser tab only.
- Nothing is booked or enrolled. Links open public websites.
- Pay is a trade-wide range, not a wage per step. Licence rules vary by state.
